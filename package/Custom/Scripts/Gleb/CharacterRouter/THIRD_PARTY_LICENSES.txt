CrossGenderHair
Creator: Stopper
Package: Stopper.CrossGenderHair
License: CC BY

CharacterRouter v80 adapts the cross-gender hair enabling technique from
Stopper's CrossGenderHair: installed CustomHairItem objects from the opposite
gender bank are cloned into the native Person hair container, then VaM's hair
items are reinitialized/resynced. Attribution: Stopper.


Morph Merge And Split - Extended
Creator: Skynet
Package: Skynet.MorphMergeAndSplit.3
License: CC BY

CharacterRouter v145 adapts the HeadFilter vertex/bone set, NeckFalloff
transition data, neck-seam correction, and MorphBuffer splitting technique
from Skynet's Morph Merge And Split - Extended v3. The adapted code is used
only inside the isolated BodyPresetAdapter to create body-only transient
morphs in memory. Attribution: Skynet.
